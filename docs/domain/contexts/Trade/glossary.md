# Trade — glossary

Terms specific to this context. Shared terms and cross-context collisions are in
[`../../glossary.md`](../../glossary.md).

| Term | Definition | Notes |
|------|------------|-------|
| Delivery Price | What a Harbor pays for the Cargo an Incoming Ship delivers: the sum of the Price of every Cargo aboard, counted as often as it is aboard. | Planned (harbor-economy, 2026-10-04). Paid from the Destination Harbor's Savings at its own Prices when it unloads the ship; becomes the ship's Earnings. A Harbor that cannot pay refuses (Refused Delivery, Shipping). The ship's own Home Harbor pays it too. |
| Earnings | The Delivery Prices a ship has been paid, carried aboard until it reaches its Home Harbor. | Planned (harbor-economy, 2026-10-04). Go into the Home Harbor's Savings when the ship arrives there. Travel with the ship between Harbors. |
| Hiring Fee | What a Harbor pays from its Savings to Hire a Recruit (Fleet). | Planned (crew ideation, 2026-10-05). In dollars ($), to two decimals. Not a Price and not a Delivery Price. Open: the amount, and whether it is the same at every Harbor. |
| Market | Where a Harbor buys Cargo into its Stock at the Cargo's Price, paid from its Savings. | Planned (harbor-economy, 2026-10-04). Part of each Harbor, not a foreign system. |
| Price | The value of one Cargo at a Harbor. | Planned (harbor-economy, 2026-10-04). In dollars ($), to two decimals. Changed 2026-10-07 (STORY-024): each Harbor rolls its own Price for every Cargo once, when it first opens — whole dollars from 30.00 $ to 60.00 $ — and keeps it, so Prices differ between Harbors. Future idea: Harbors announce their Prices to the other Harbors. Not to be confused with Shipping Quote (Shipping), which is a saying. |
| Savings | The money a Harbor holds. | Planned (harbor-economy, 2026-10-04). In dollars ($), to two decimals. Pays Delivery Prices and Market purchases; receives the Earnings of ships whose Home Harbor it is. |
| Starting Savings | The Savings a Harbor opens with. | Planned (harbor-economy, 2026-10-04). 1000 $, the same at every Harbor. |
